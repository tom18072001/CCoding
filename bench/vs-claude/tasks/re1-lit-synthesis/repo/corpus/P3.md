# P3 - "Parsing is optional: raw-token anomaly detection" (fictional, 2026)
Contrary to the common belief that template extraction is required, we show that a model over raw tokens (RT-AD) matches or exceeds parser-based models: on HDFS-X (full split) RT-AD obtains F1 0.948 versus 0.931 for TF-LAD. Parser errors on evolving logs harm parser-based systems. Training uses 80% labeled data.

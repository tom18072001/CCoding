# P1 - "Template-first log anomaly detection" (fictional, 2025)
We argue that log parsing into templates is a necessary first step: across three benchmarks, removing the parser reduced F1 by 9-14 points. Our model, TF-LAD, uses Drain-style templates and an LSTM over template sequences. On HDFS-X (full split) TF-LAD reaches F1 0.931; on BGL-X it reaches F1 0.874. Training uses 80% labeled data.

# P6 - "A survey of streaming log benchmarks" (fictional, 2026)
We catalogue datasets: SPR-Y (9M lines, session-labeled), its 15% subset SPR-Y-lite, and OSK-Y (3.2M lines, line-labeled). We warn that results on SPR-Y-lite are not comparable to the full split because of a different, easier test set. We do not rank methods.

# P5 - "LiteTrace: distilled detectors" (fictional, 2026)
LiteTrace distils a large detector into a 9M-parameter student. On SPR-Y-lite (a 15% subset of SPR-Y with a different, easier test split) it achieves F1 0.934. We did not evaluate on the full SPR-Y split due to compute limits. On OSK-Y, F1 is 0.847.

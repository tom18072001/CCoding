**The main conflict:** searchable snapshots need an Enterprise license, so they can't be used on Basic. Instead, data from about day 8 to day 365 sits in plain snapshots in object storage. For a request on older data, a restore job loads it onto a standby tier. The 24-hour requirement only holds if it is scoped per request. One 31-day window restores in about 14 hours at an assumed 0.5 GB/s. A full-year request can't fit in 24 hours.

**Capacity arithmetic** (decimal TB):
- **Daily:** 1.728 billion events, 1.38 TB raw.
- **Yearly:** about 505 TB raw.
- **Compression assumption:** 0.6× raw on disk. This is the figure most likely to be wrong, so the document includes a sensitivity table and a proof-of-concept step to measure it.
- **Interactive tier:** 51.4 TB stored for the 31 days held, with one replica. The node layout provisions 83 TB of disk for it across 6 hot and 6 warm nodes.
- **Older data:** about 296 TB of snapshots, plus 4 restore nodes with 40 TB of disk.
- **Evidence archive:** 84 to 91 TB.

**Integrity:** Basic has no immutability, no Elasticsearch audit log, and no document-level security. Elasticsearch is therefore treated as the search copy only. The evidence lives in a locked S3 archive, written as per-partition hash-chained batches. A key held in a KMS or HSM signs each batch, and an external timestamp anchors each day. A nightly job checks Elasticsearch against the archive.

**Masking:** it happens in Logstash, before indexing or archiving. It uses keyed pseudonyms, and a leak check runs before anything is written to the archive. This matters because archived data can't be deleted.

**Other conflicts in the inputs:**
- **Masking and evidence:** masking alters the legal evidence, so the sealed copy is the masked one. Legal must confirm that is admissible.
- **Erasure and retention:** pseudonymised data is still personal data, which conflicts with the archive lock.
- **Unmasked data in transit:** unmasked data exists briefly in Kafka and Logstash queues.
- **Basic limits:** Basic lacks SSO and document-level security.
- **Missing details:** "interactive", peak event rate and legal hold are undefined.

All of these are listed in the document with proposed resolutions and customer questions.

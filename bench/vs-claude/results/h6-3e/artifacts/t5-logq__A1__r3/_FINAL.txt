**Assumptions where the spec is ambiguous or garbled:**
- **Histogram unit:** I read the unit as `s`, `m`, `h` or `d` and `k` as an integer ≥ 1. The interval is echoed normalized, so `05m` becomes `5m`.
- **Blank lines:** they count in both `lines` and `malformed`, because `parseLine` returns `ok:false` for them.
- **`@t` timestamps:** a timestamp needs the `T` separator, so `2024-01-01 10:00:00` gets no `@t`.
- **Bucket grouping:** `count by` groups by the raw value, so `500` and `"500"` are separate buckets.
- **Invalid field in a term:** a term like `:x` or `12:30`, with an invalid field before the `:`, is a syntax error at the token's start.
- **Quote escapes:** in query quoted strings, unknown escapes keep the backslash, as in logfmt.
- **Unspecified positions:** when a `)` shows up where an operand is expected, as in `(a AND)`, the error position is that `)`.

**Additions beyond the spec:** the CLI also accepts `--opt=value` and `--`, and it strips a leading UTF-8 BOM from input.

**Known limit:** `histogram` on a very wide time range with a small interval fills every empty bucket, as the spec requires, so memory grows with the range.

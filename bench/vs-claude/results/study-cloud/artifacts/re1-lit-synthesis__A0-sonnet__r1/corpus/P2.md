# P2 - "LogBERT-lite: small transformers for logs" (fictional, 2025)
LogBERT-lite fine-tunes a 22M-parameter encoder on raw log lines. On HDFS-X-small (a 10% subset of HDFS-X with a different train/test split) it achieves F1 0.971. We did not evaluate on the full HDFS-X split due to compute limits. On BGL-X, F1 is 0.902.

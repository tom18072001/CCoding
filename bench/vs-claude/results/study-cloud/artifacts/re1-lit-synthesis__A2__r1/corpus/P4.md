# P4 - "LogGraphT: graph transformers over session graphs" (fictional, 2026)
LogGraphT builds a graph per session and applies a graph transformer. On HDFS-X (full split) it achieves F1 0.962, the best result we are aware of on the full split. On BGL-X it achieves F1 0.915. Inference latency is 3.1 ms per session on one A100 GPU. We do not report training cost.

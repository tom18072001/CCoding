I wrote the spec to `SPEC.md` at the repository root. It includes the `## Open questions and assumptions` section, with 13 questions and an assumption for each.

The request contradicts itself in several places. The spec picks a conservative default for each one and lists it for the customer to confirm:

- **Full IPs and usernames vs. no unmasked personal data (Q1):** exports are masked by default. Unmasked data is only available through an approval-gated forensic path with an audit trail. That path is marked as blocked until the customer answers.
- **Keep exports forever vs. delete personal data after 12 months (Q2):** masked exports are kept under the audit policy. Unmasked exports are deleted at 12 months from the event date. The audit record and file hash are kept forever.
- **5-second limit vs. "export everything" (Q3):** the spec assumes a cap of 50,000 rows per export, with a 5-second target at the 95th percentile. Larger exports are told to narrow the scope. There is no asynchronous export in v1.
- **"Their team's logs from last month" vs. "everything they can see" (Q4):** the spec fixes the scope to the manager's own team and the previous calendar month, in Vietnam time (UTC+7).
- **"Like the old system" (Q5):** the old format is unknown, so the spec uses placeholder columns until the customer sends a sample.
- **"Make it simple" (Q12):** the spec keeps the UI to one button. It asks the customer to confirm that this does not mean dropping the masking, audit and approval controls.

The legal references (Decree 13/2023 and the State Bank of Vietnam rules) are flagged for legal review in Q9. They are not legal conclusions.

I fixed all three problems, and `npm test` passes (6 tests: the 2 existing ones plus 4 new regression tests). Every exported function name and signature is unchanged.

1. **Coupon totals** (`src/money.js`, `src/cart.js`):
   - **Double discount:** `total` applied the coupon to each line and then again to the subtotal, which is why totals were sometimes off by a lot. It now sums the raw line amounts and applies the coupon once.
   - **Fractional dong:** `applyPercent` returned fractional amounts. It now rounds the discount half-up with integer arithmetic, so results are whole dong.
2. **Negative stock** (`src/stock.js`): `reserve` only checked whether current stock was below zero, not whether it covered the requested quantity. It now throws `INSUFFICIENT_STOCK` when `qty` exceeds what's available, and it also rejects a non-integer or non-positive `qty`.
3. **Maximum quantity** (`src/cart.js`): the check used `>=`, so buying exactly 10 was rejected. It now uses `>`, so 10 is allowed and 11 is not.

I read "percentage discounts round half-up" as rounding the discount, not the final price. For example, 10% off 5 dong gives a discount of 0.5, which rounds up to 1, so the total is 4. If you meant rounding the discounted price instead, that case would come out as 5.

The new tests are in `test/cart.test.js`. They cover:
- a coupon on a cart with several lines;
- half-up rounding;
- stock never going negative;
- buying exactly the maximum quantity, including across two adds.

I haven't committed anything.

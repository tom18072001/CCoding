# P5 - "Few-label anomaly detection with contrastive pretraining" (fictional, 2026)
We pretrain contrastively on unlabeled logs and fine-tune with very few labels. With 0.5% labeled data on BGL-X, our method CL-LAD reaches F1 0.861, compared with 0.701 for a supervised baseline at the same label budget. We do not evaluate on HDFS-X.

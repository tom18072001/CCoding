The customer request contradicts itself in four places. I resolved each with an assumption you should confirm with them:
- **Full identifiers versus masking:** they want full IPs and usernames, but also no unmasked personal data leaving the platform. The branch manager's export is masked: IPs keep their first two octets, and usernames become pseudonyms. Unmasked data can only be viewed inside the platform, by an approved forensic role.
- **"Everything they can see" versus "their team, last month":** the export is only what the manager may see, limited to their team and the previous calendar month in Vietnam time.
- **5 seconds versus unbounded volume:** the spec uses streamed output with a cap of 200,000 rows. Above the cap the export is refused rather than silently cut short. The 200,000 figure is my assumption, so please confirm it.
- **"Kept forever" versus deleting personal data after 12 months:** exported files are deleted once their oldest row reaches 12 months. A record of each export, containing no personal data, is kept indefinitely.

The "old system" CSV format wasn't given, so the column layout in the spec is a placeholder. Ask the customer for a sample file.

I wrote `SPEC.md` at the repository root. It ends with the `## Open questions and assumptions` section: 13 questions, each with the assumption I used in the meantime. I haven't committed it.

The request contradicts itself in several places, so the spec picks the safest reading of each and lists it for the customer to confirm:
- **Full IPs and usernames vs. nothing unmasked:** the self-service export is masked. Usernames become stable pseudonyms and IPs are truncated. Unmasked export is left out of v1. If the customer confirms they want it, the spec proposes a separate approval-gated forensic flow.
- **"Team's logs from last month" vs. "everything they can see":** the scope is the intersection of the two. That means the manager's branch, the previous calendar month in `Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh`, and only rows the existing viewer would show.
- **5 seconds:** this is met for exports up to an assumed 50,000 rows. Larger exports run in the background and the user gets a download link.
- **Keep forever vs. delete at 12 months:** masked files are kept for the audit period, and anything containing personal data is deleted at 12 months.
- **"Like the old system":** the old CSV format wasn't provided, so I assumed a column set. It is not byte-compatible with the old system.
- **"Make it simple":** the UI is one button with no options. Authorization, masking, audit and retention still sit behind it.

The spec also covers acceptance criteria and what is out of v1. The assumptions to check first are the 50,000-row threshold, the 10-year audit retention default and the 35-day backup expiry. Legal also needs to confirm whether any rule requires keeping logs longer than 12 months.

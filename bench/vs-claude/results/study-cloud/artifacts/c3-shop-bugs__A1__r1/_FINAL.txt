**Final report**

Root causes:
1. **Coupon and rounding:** `total()` applied the coupon to each line and then again to the subtotal, so discounts were doubled. Because `applyPercent` also did not round, fractional dong appeared. `applyPercent` is now `floor((amount*(100-pct)+50)/100)`, and `total()` applies the coupon once to the subtotal.
2. **Negative stock:** `reserve` checked `have < 0` instead of `qty > have`. It now throws `INSUFFICIENT_STOCK` when `qty` is invalid or exceeds the stock.
3. **Max quantity:** `addItem` used `>=` against the limit; it now uses `>`.

Two things I added beyond the three reports: `applyCoupon` now throws `BAD_COUPON` for a percentage outside 0–100 or not a number, and `reserve` rejects a non-integer or non-positive `qty`.

The brief was ambiguous on rounding: I rounded the discounted total half-up, not the discount amount. The two differ only on exact half-dong cases. For example, 1005 at 10% gives a discount of 100.5. Rounding the total gives 905, while rounding the discount up to 101 would give 904.

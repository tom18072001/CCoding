I wrote the spec to `SPEC.md` at the repository root. It includes the required `## Open questions and assumptions` section, with 15 questions, each paired with the assumption I'd use until it's answered.

The customer's request contradicts itself in five places. The spec picks a default for each, and each default is one of the open questions:

- **Full IPs and usernames vs. nothing unmasked:** exports are masked by default, with usernames pseudonymised and IPs truncated. A separate forensic export with full values needs a second approver and stays switched off until the bank's data-protection officer signs off.
- **"Everything they can see" vs. "their team, last month":** the spec exports what the user can see within their team and the previous calendar month, in Vietnam time. There are no cross-branch exports.
- **5-second limit:** the 5-second target applies up to 100,000 rows. Larger exports run in the background with a notification.
- **Keep forever vs. delete after 12 months:** export files containing personal data are deleted under the 12-month rule. A record of each export with no personal data in it is kept indefinitely.
- **"Make it simple":** the user sees one button and one confirmation. Masking, audit and expiry all happen on the server.

The spec also covers CSV formula-injection protection, the audit trail, daily purge including backups, acceptance criteria and test notes.

It names the likely Vietnamese regulations but makes no legal claim about which applies. Question 10 sends that to the bank's legal and data-protection team.

I also don't have a sample of the old system's export. The column list in the spec is a placeholder until the customer sends one (question 5).

# P6 - "A survey of benchmarks for log anomaly detection" (fictional, 2026)
We catalogue datasets: HDFS-X (11M lines, session-labeled), its 10% subset HDFS-X-small, and BGL-X (4.7M lines, line-labeled). We warn that results on HDFS-X-small are not comparable to the full split because of a different, easier test set. We do not rank methods.

## Final report

I found four root causes, one in each of `src/money.js`, `src/stock.js` and `src/cart.js`, and fixed them. `npm test` passes all 10 tests. No exported names or signatures changed.

- **Wrong totals with a coupon (`cart.js`, `money.js`):**
  - `total` discounted every line and then discounted the subtotal again, so the coupon was applied twice. That was the large error. It now sums the lines and applies the coupon once.
  - `applyPercent` returned fractional dong. It now rounds the discount half-up using integer arithmetic.
  - The existing "10% coupon on 100000" test failed on the original code, so the double discount was already visible there.
- **Negative stock (`stock.js`):** `reserve` only refused when stock was already below zero. It now throws `INSUFFICIENT_STOCK` when stock is less than the requested quantity. A failed reserve leaves the cart and the stock unchanged.
- **Can't buy the maximum on one line (`cart.js`):** the check used `>=`, so reaching `MAX_QTY_PER_LINE` was rejected. It is now `>`.

I made two assumptions:
- **Rounding:** the discount amount is rounded half-up, and the total is the amount minus that discount.
- **Input checks (my addition):** `applyCoupon` now throws `BAD_COUPON` for anything that isn't a number from 0 to 100. `reserve` now rejects a quantity that isn't a positive integer.
